# Avara discovery product

Status: proposed direction, November 2026. Avara remains the working brand; no domain or repository rename is implied.

## Positioning
Answer "Where should I live and what will it cost?" Avara Explore covers property listings and editorial neighbourhood discovery; Avara Intelligence covers transparent calculators and eventually evidence-backed market comparisons; Avara Property OS is the separate operational software. The public surface is account-optional, not a SaaS onboarding funnel.

## Initial scope
- Residential rentals in a narrow Colombo-area pilot; maintain existing sale/stay routes for compatibility.
- Five neighbourhood guides: Colombo, Nugegoda, Maharagama, Rajagiriya, Dehiwala. Phase 1 directory links only; publish substantive guides after source and editorial review.
- 30 permissioned listings/references and five agent conversations; refresh inventory weekly.
- Personal monthly living cost tool, followed by rent/buy scenarios, perches/square feet conversion, and asking-price comparison.
- Consented direct enquiries; no in-platform payment required for discovery.
- Property OS: one secondary section linking directly to its GitHub repository; packaging and release distribution deferred.
- CMS-managed editorial home sections, guides, collections, partner information and navigation with draft/preview/publish workflow.
- Do not scrape or republish listings, photos or personal details without authorization.

## Differentiation: Total Living Cost Index
Use separately sourced rent, utilities, commute assumptions and amenities. Disclose observation period, sample size, geography, asking versus transaction price, exclusions and coverage. Normalize currency, rental periods and land units; deduplicate observations. Suppress sparse samples and don't generate market figures. User-entered scenarios are separate from measured data. No invented verification, rankings, customer counts or price claims.

## Monetization experiments (not live prices)
Basic listing free; featured LKR 1,500/30 days; agent LKR 4,500/month; accepted qualified enquiry LKR 500; negotiated disclosed referrals (Blu services, connectivity, furniture). Five agents on a 30-day free pilot; only charge after measurable leads. Separate sponsored placements from organic recommendations. Track consented enquiries, accepted leads, freshness, renewal and partner referral conversion.

Property OS remains independently operated. No shared tenancy/financial data or automatic account coupling. Blu and FreshPick can be linked only for relevant, disclosed referrals.
